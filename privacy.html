Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Store

Quadton Bot stores essential identifiers required for economy functionality, including Discord User IDs, Usernames, Wallet/Bank balances, Inventory items, and Level progression stats.

2. Data Usage & Security

Stored data is exclusively used to maintain Quad-Coins balances, inventories, and career progression. We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.

3. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request complete account data deletion by executing resetting commands or contacting server support administrators.
